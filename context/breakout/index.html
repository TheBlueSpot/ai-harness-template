Breakout: Gravity Well

Gravity aim is the whole trick. Live play keeps the HUD thin, so the reminder stays in this quick-help panel instead of on the field.

  • Move mouse to bend the ball toward openings.
  • Hold mouse for stronger pull and extra side-spin.
  • Click, space, or enter launches and restarts.
  • Press H for help, or Esc / click away to close it.
