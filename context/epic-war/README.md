# Epic War: Summoner Siege

Three-lane siege game about spending mana on units, timing spells, and holding the castle until the enemy keep falls.

## Hook

You defend your castle by spending mana on units, firing cooldown spells into the lane under pressure, and timing upgrades before the enemy push snowballs.

## Play

- Open `./index.html` in a browser to play locally.
- Start a run from the menu and hold all three lanes until the enemy keep falls.
- Use lane focus, spell timing, and gold upgrades to stabilize losing fronts before they collapse.
