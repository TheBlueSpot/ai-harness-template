Paperboy Route

Ride three lanes, line up curbside boxes, and throw papers with space. The street scrolls on its own, so each lane change has to happen early.

The first block teaches the first mailbox up close. After that, dogs, drains, and traffic force cleaner route planning without cluttering the play space.
