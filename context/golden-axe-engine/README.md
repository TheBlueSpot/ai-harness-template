# Golden Axe Engine

Standalone browser belt-scroller focused on lane control, crowd pressure, mount theft, and screen-clearing magic timing.

## Loop

- Push through three short combat stages.
- Strafe up and down the lane plane to break enemy flanks.
- Defeat mounted raiders, then claim the dropped beast for stronger movement and melee reach.
- Fill the magic meter through hits and kills, then spend it for a full-screen special.

## Controls

- `WASD` or arrow keys: move across the arena and lane depth
- `J`: melee attack
- `K`: cast magic when the meter is full
- `E`: mount or dismount nearby beasts
- `Enter`: start or restart a run

## Notes

- Open [index.html](./index.html) in a browser to play.
- The loop gets stickier when flank pressure, mount steals, and magic timing overlap without turning the lane read into clutter.
