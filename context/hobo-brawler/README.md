# Hobo: Brawl Tactics

Browser-playable alley brawler focused on short input strings, lane control, and crowd-manipulation combos.

Open [index.html](./index.html) in a browser to play.

## What It Is

This entry turns a back-alley brawl into a compact combo-routing game. The player pressures a small crowd, grabs isolated targets, and uses launches to convert one opening into a wider clear.

## Play Feel

- Movement stays readable on a narrow belt-scroller plane.
- Essential controls stay close to the action instead of relying on a bulky tutorial layer.
- Retry friction is low so failed runs stay inside the learning loop.

## Session Goal

Clear the alley by chaining punches, grab conversions, and launch follow-ups before the crowd collapses your spacing.
