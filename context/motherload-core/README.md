# Motherload Core Breach

Motherload Core Breach is browser mining run about diving through unstable strata, grabbing richer ore under pressure, and limping back to surface dock before ship turns into scrap.

## Controls

- `WASD` or arrow keys to thrust
- `R` to launch run from title or restart after loss
- `1` to refuel at dock
- `2` to repair hull at dock
- `3` to upgrade cargo bay at dock
- `4` to upgrade drill at dock

## Play Loop

- Carve descent tunnels through dirt, ore, and deep rock
- Watch fuel, hull, pressure, and cargo weight while climbing back up
- Sell ore automatically when you settle on dock pad
- Spend credits on fuel, repairs, cargo, and stronger drill passes
- Hit depth target and credit target, then return to dock to clear run

## Local Notes

- Entry stays self-contained inside `motherload-core/`
- Open `motherload-core/index.html` in browser to play
- Dock panel only activates while landed on surface pad
- Cargo weight lowers thrust and top speed until you cash out
