# Oregon Trail Crossing

Oregon Trail Crossing is a browser-playable overland survival run about keeping a wagon crew alive long enough to reach the valley. The loop stays simple: travel for miles, tune pace and rations, stop to hunt for food, and clear river crossings with a timing-based skill check before attrition catches up.

## Play

Open `./oregon-trail-crossing/index.html` in a desktop browser.

## Controls

- Click `Travel`, `Hunt`, `Rest`, `Change Pace`, and `Change Rations` on the right rail.
- During hunting, click deer to spend ammo and collect food.
- River crossings start automatically when the wagon reaches a river; click the playfield or press `Space` when the marker sits inside the safe band.

## Loop

- Travel pushes the wagon toward the finish but consumes food every day.
- Hunting is the fastest way to refill food, but missed shots waste ammo.
- River crossings are the main fail point: a clean timing hit preserves supplies, while a bad hit costs health, morale, and cargo.
