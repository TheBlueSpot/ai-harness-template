Turn-based settlement defense

Rebuild: City Management

Keep the perimeter standing through a two-phase loop. In daylight you assign survivors to sectors. At night the city resolves scavenging, repairs, defense, and zombie breaches across the border.

Plan Pick a tile, then assign a survivor from the roster.
Night Breach probability rises with noise, danger, and weak walls.
Lose The run ends when food is gone or the perimeter collapses.
