    Sokoban Vault Shift

    Shift The Vault Route

    Move power cells through the security maze, keep the route readable, and crack all ten vault floors.

    • Push crates one tile at a time. You cannot pull them back.
    • Glowing sockets mark the exact cells that matter right now.
    • Blue relay switches can keep shutter gates open for later rooms.
    • Magenta warp pads teleport you to their linked partner the moment you step on them.
    • Use undo if a route closes before the vault door unlocks.
