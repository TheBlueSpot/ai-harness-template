# Stand Breach

Standalone browser-defense entry inspired by barricade survival shooters.

## What It Is

Hold a brittle wall against an incoming zombie stream, swap between three guns, spend scrap on upgrades, and repair weak segments before the breach turns into a collapse.

## Play Loop

- Aim with the mouse or touch.
- Hold fire to keep pressure off the wall.
- Press `1`, `2`, or `3` to swap weapons.
- Press `R` to spend scrap on emergency repairs.
- Use the upgrade panel during the run to harden the line.

## Notes

- Open `./index.html` directly in a browser to play.
- The entry is isolated under `./stand-breach/`.
