# Sword Souls Train

Browser-playable training-and-arena prototype focused on quick stat growth, readable combat outcomes, and direct folder boot.

## Play

Open [index.html](./index.html) in a browser to play.

## What It Is

The player trains a fighter through three lightweight drills, then enters the arena against escalating enemies. Training feeds live combat stats immediately, so the loop stays short and legible.

## Core Loop

- Complete a drill to raise combat readiness.
- Enter the arena and pick an encounter.
- Spend simple actions in combat and read the battle log.
- Return to training or queue another fight.

## Notes

- Keeps the HUD compact and puts combat feedback in the main arena flow.
