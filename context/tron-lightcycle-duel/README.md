# Tron Lightcycle Duel

Arcade arena rider about trapping rival bikes inside their own light walls.

Open [index.html](./index.html) in a browser to play.

## Loop

- Steer on the grid and leave a permanent trail behind your bike.
- Burn boost cells to cut sharper angles and seize empty space before rivals do.
- Clear four quick rounds with near-instant rematch flow.

## Controls

- `Arrow keys`: turn
- `Space`: boost
- `Enter`: start, continue, rematch

## Notes

- Four short rounds and instant rematches keep the loop focused on reading space and closing traps fast.
- Open [index.html](./index.html) in a browser to play.
